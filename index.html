Teaching Framework

From proficiency to goals to instruction.

Language development is ongoing and student-centered. Instruction should build confidence, access, and independence.

01

Understand Students

Use language data as a reference, not a label, and identify strengths across listening, speaking, reading, and writing.

02

Build Reflection

Help students understand their learning process, discuss strengths, and build confidence through reflection.

03

Set Language Goals

Support students in setting clear, meaningful goals and choosing strategies that help them grow.
